# ADR 0010: Subagent model precedence

## Status
Active

## Context
Specialist settings outrank generic defaults.

## Decision
Supersedes ADR0009. Model/effort priority: explicit user choice, specialist/skill settings, then gpt-6.1-sol/low. No forks. Contract2.0: blockers and mandatory final send_message; wait 1800000 ms; at most three independent active descendants; root may request material efficiency analysis.

## Consequences
Native defaults stay unchanged; generic selection is fallback.
