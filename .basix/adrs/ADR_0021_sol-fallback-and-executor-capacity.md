# ADR 0021: Sol fallback and capacity

## Status
Active

## Context
Keep Sol current.

## Decision
Supersedes ADR 0015. Model/effort: user, specialist/skill, newest dispatch-supported Sol/low. No forks. Contract 2.0: blockers and mandatory final via send_message. Wait 1800000 ms. Max 3 executors, each with 2 read-only exploration/research children. Max 6 read-only helpers and 9 descendants, subject to runtime limits. Root may request material efficiency analysis.

## Consequences
Native defaults stay.
