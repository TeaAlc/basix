## Semantic decisions and testable proposals

These are fictional design proposals for root/product acceptance at `bind`, not measured external facts. Replay is authoritative for deterministic domain state from accepted commands; committed shared-database versions/receipts are authoritative for acceptance. Allocation owns reservation capacity/epoch/lifecycle; execution's committed admission/outcome owns dispatch consumption, and carrier lookup by stable key resolves an external operation's unknown outcome. Settlement's immutable balanced ledger owns financial postings. Broker messages, local caches and HTTP timeouts are observations, never authority to release or charge. Conflicts stop/reconcile the affected aggregate with reason and causal context.

One pure shared engine performs every service's domain decisions, receives time/IDs/policy versions explicitly and has no service, DB, network, random or wall-clock dependency. Canonicalization includes stable ordering and effect identity. Checked integer quantities have explicit units; money uses integer minor units with tariff version and per-currency balancing. Deterministic candidate tie-breaking, reservation revisions and monotonic fence checks are shared contracts. Engine replay can reproduce effect identities but external effect delivery is reconciled through durable receipts and carrier/ledger identities rather than reexecuted blindly.

All tenant-owned primary/foreign/unique keys, queries and locks include tenant. Explicit predicates plus fail-closed DB isolation, context cleanup after success/error/pool return and aggregate-bounded conditional locking are mandatory. Bad/missing tenant fails before business reads. Aggregate revision, canonical journal, inbox receipt and generated outbox append commit atomically. Outbox publish occurs after commit, with bounded fenced claims; crashes on both sides of publish/ack are safe. Envelopes retain tenant/event ID/producer/aggregate ID and version/schema revision/causation/correlation/occurred time across retries. Duplicate identical identity returns the same logical result; conflicting identity quarantines with reason. Gap handling buffers bounded pending revisions and reconciles the aggregate rather than applying dependent facts out of order or manufacturing stock.

Propose retry policy: 5 attempts within 60 seconds, exponential backoff starting 100 ms capped 5 seconds with injected deterministic jitter seed; persist exhaustion with tenant, original identity, causal context and reason. Reconciliation: up to 100 missing revisions / 60 seconds per aggregate per pass, then durable blocked state and audited operator decision; unrelated aggregates continue. Redrive preserves original identity and rechecks invariants. Propose lease/claim duration 30 seconds, renewal at 10 seconds, fencing on every conditional write and before carrier admission; expiry does not establish dispatch rejection. Authority validation/renewal has bounded 2-second timeout and never recursively calls back into the original request; unknown outcomes enter durable reconciliation and carrier-key lookup before retry/release. Expired/cancelled reservations cannot release consumed dispatch capacity. Stop stale owners from both carrier operations and finalization. Validate values with injected clocks, network loss, crash and scheduler delay; tune only with root-approved fixtures/policy versions.

Propose journal retention minimum 30 days, **extended without limit by safety holds** for snapshot verification, unacknowledged outbox/inbox work, unresolved dispatch or ledger recovery and audited retention requirements. No timer overrides a safety hold. Snapshot lineage includes tenant/aggregate, engine format, journal boundary and digest. Wrong lineage, digest mismatch or truncation must verify a complete recoverable journal fallback or visibly stop only that aggregate.

Propose v1 coexistence/old-binary rollback window 7 days after compatible release, restartable backfill batches of 500 records per tenant capped at 1 second lock time with version-CAS conflict retry under the retry budget. Propose readiness/degraded observation within 5 seconds of peer outage and 60-second recovery fixture deadline after peer restoration. These and the fictional fairness fixture (100 competing hot-tenant requests, ten independent second-tenant requests completed within 5 seconds) need a declared 4-vCPU/8-GiB fixture host, local test DB/broker/simulator, scheduler and load assumptions, and repeated deterministic runs; they are acceptance-test proposals, not capacity claims. Bind must accept or replace unspecified budgets, units, currency rounding/tariff rules, gap authority, canonical encoding, retry/retention obligations, outage domains, rollout/restore objectives and retirement ownership before related behavior is implemented. Product ambiguity blocks only affected work; root records decisions and replans genuine durable architecture via applicable ADR workflow.
